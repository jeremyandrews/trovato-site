On the pinned kernel `/` is served by the ordinary item route, which renders the page through the first of `page--item--` and `page` that exists. Until 0.105.0 a separate front page handler rendered it as `page--front`, so this file was called `page--front.html`; on 0.105.0 nothing asks for that name any more, the page fell through to page.html, and page.html printed a breadcrumb ("Home > Front Page > trovato") above the hero.
