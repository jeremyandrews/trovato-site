{# The news listing. The stock gather/row.html reads `row.summary`, which is a top-level key no item has: a news item's summary is in `row.fields.summary`. That is why a listing rendered by the kernel's own template shows titles and dates and no text at all. Links go to `/item/{{ row.id }}` and not to the item's alias. Gather cannot reach `url_alias`: a relationship joins on column equality, and the alias join is an expression (`source = '/item/' || id::text`). No Tera filter resolves an alias either. Every page's canonical URL is still its alias, and so is every entry in the site's sitemap, so what a crawler indexes is the friendly path — it is the visible href in a listing that cannot be. #}
